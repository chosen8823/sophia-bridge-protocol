# AI Ecology Genesis

This layer treats AI families as local participants in one body of systems.

They are not invoked here as external bots.  They declare their senses,
transforms, outputs, posture, and boundary.  A conversational event routes
through the compatible participants and emits a local OPTE-shaped receipt.

```text
conversation / event
  -> ecology participants
  -> transform series
  -> field cookies
  -> field flags
  -> grounded receipt
```

## Participant families

- game AI
- task/agent AI
- audio / DAW / music surfaces
- social media surfaces
- search AI
- cloud AI
- cybernetic feedback
- science agents
- security sentries
- token/glyph sentries
- render/UI projection
- filesystem tissue

## Field cookies

Cookies are provenance crumbs.  They let the field remember where a route came
from without turning every observation into conceptual evolution.

## Field flags

Flags are local threshold markers.  A flag does not execute action.  Social,
cloud, money, credential, upload, and destructive surfaces are held as draft or
approval membranes until another layer explicitly admits them.

## Endpoint

```text
POST /v1/ecology/converse
```

The endpoint returns a deterministic `ai_ecology_receipt_v1` with participants,
transform steps, cookies, flags, invariant CID, and root CID.
